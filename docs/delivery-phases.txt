Final Architecture Principle
The platform should help users understand and organize their finances without presenting deterministic insights as guaranteed outcomes or autonomous financial management.
The authoritative flow remains:
USER FINANCIAL DATA
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC FINANCIAL ENGINE
        ↓
CALCULATED FACTS
        ↓
CONFIGURABLE FINANCIAL RULES
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC EXPLANATIONS + POSSIBLE STRATEGIES
        ↓
USER DECISION
        ↓
APPROVED FINANCIAL PLAN
        ↓
PAYDAY / RECURRING ROUTINES
        ↓
ACTUAL USER ACTIONS
        ↓
TRACKING + ANALYTICS
        ↓
NEXT REVIEW
This separation makes the MVP implementable by a small team while preserving a path toward more sophisticated analytics, additional countries, native mobile applications and future integrations.
