Personal Finance & Wealth Management System
Background
Many working people earn regular or irregular income but struggle to convert that income into savings, investments, emergency reserves and long-term wealth because they lack structured financial-management tools and practical guidance.
The proposed platform is a personal financial-management and wealth-building system initially focused on Ghana while being architected for additional countries and currencies.
The initial target users are salaried and self-employed individuals who need help understanding:
•	where their money goes;
•	how much they can reasonably save;
•	their debts and liabilities;
•	their current net worth;
•	progress toward financial goals;
•	the performance and structure of their investments;
•	what financial actions they could consider next.
The system is primarily an information, planning, tracking and decision-support platform.
It will not hold customer funds, execute investments or transfer money in the MVP. Users continue using their existing banks, Mobile Money services, brokers, fund managers and investment platforms.
Financial data is manually entered during the MVP. Bank integrations, Mobile Money integrations, CSV/bank-statement imports and automatic transaction synchronization are explicitly excluded.
The system's core design principle is:
Financial Engine calculates → Rules detect → AI explains/recommends → User decides → System tracks
AI must never become the authoritative calculator for balances, returns, interest, net worth or portfolio metrics.

12. Portfolio Analytics Engine
Analytics operate on transactions, valuations and historical FX rates.
The engine calculates:
•	absolute return;
•	percentage return;
•	annualized return;
•	TWR;
•	XIRR;
•	asset allocation;
•	concentration;
•	diversification;
•	volatility;
•	contribution analysis;
•	investment income;
•	maturity exposure;
•	historical comparisons;
•	goal alignment;
•	projections.
TWR divides performance into valuation periods and geometrically links period returns:
TWR = Π(1 + period_return) - 1
XIRR solves:
Σ CF_i / (1 + r)^((date_i-date_0)/365) = 0
Use a bounded numerical solver with:
•	validated cash-flow inputs;
•	maximum iterations;
•	convergence tolerance;
•	fallback failure state;
•	unit tests against independently verified examples.
Do not fabricate an XIRR when convergence fails.
Initial risk analytics include volatility, concentration and diversification.
Sharpe ratio, beta, alpha and VaR are postponed until dependable benchmark/risk-free/market datasets exist.
Maturity buckets:
0–30 days
31–90 days
91–180 days
181–365 days
> 1 year
Every important metric supports:
How is this calculated?
Data used
Measurement period
Limitations
Data freshness
When insufficient information exists, return:
NOT_ENOUGH_DATA
rather than inventing a result.
